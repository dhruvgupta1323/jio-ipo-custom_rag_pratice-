Jio Platforms Limited IPO 
 
Incorporated on November 15, 2019, Jio Platforms Limited is a technology company focused 
on digital connectivity, digital services and technology-led solutions in India. The Company 
has developed proprietary digital technology and pan-India connectivity infrastructure to 
support the delivery of digital services to consumers and businesses. Its subsidiary, Reliance 
Jio Infocomm Limited, served 533.3 million customers as of June 30, 2026.The Company 
operates through an integrated technology platform encompassing digital connectivity, 
consumer applications, enterprise solutions, cloud services, Internet of Things (IoT) and 
artificial intelligence (AI). Its consumer offerings include mobile and fixed broadband, digital 
entertainment, cloud storage, virtual PC services and smart home solutions. For businesses, it 
provides enterprise connectivity, cloud and security solutions, managed services, IoT 
applications and AI-based solutions. The Company’s proprietary technology capabilities span 
network infrastructure, device engineering, software, operating systems and digital 
applications. 
The Company’s infrastructure includes pan-India 4G and 5G connectivity, fibre connectivity 
across more than 1,200 facilities and over 100 edge nodes within its networks as of June 30, 
2026. Its wireless broadband network covered more than 99% of India’s population. The 
Company and its subsidiaries had filed 7,220 patent applications, of which 1,043 had been 
granted, as of June 30, 2026. Its technology initiatives include an indigenous 5G technology 
stack, proprietary AI platform JioBrain and fixed wireless access technology. The Company 
also leverages a nationwide distribution network and partnerships to expand access to digital 
services. 
As of June 30, 2026, the Company had 27,932 full-time employees and engaged 63,795 
personnel on a contract basis. Its workforce includes teams across digital products and 
technology development, business operations, sales and distribution, customer service, network 
operations, and corporate functions. 
Competitive Strengths 
• 
Leadership in digital connectivity and services across India. 
• 
Diversified portfolio of consumer and enterprise digital services. 
• 
Large and highly engaged customer base with nationwide reach. 
• 
Nationwide digital distribution network and strategic group synergies.
